This is the **shared** packet for the whole class. Everyone works from the same
evidence. Your grade comes from your **reasoning** and your **3-minute recorded
defense** — not from having unique numbers. Do not just restate these files;
diagnose them, decide, and be ready to defend every claim on camera.

**The service:** the same TrustBank fraud API you built in Project 2 and scaled in
Homework 4. It has now been running in production for 12 weeks.

**Files (read in this order):**
1. `monitoring_metrics.csv` / `.md` — 12 weeks of production monitoring for the model in production
2. `test_governance_inventory.md` — what testing/monitoring/governance TrustBank currently has (and doesn't)
3. `ab_results.csv` / `.md` — a shadow A/B: the current model vs. a retrained candidate
4. `model_card.md` — a **partial** model card you must critique and complete
5. `declines.csv` — 10 transactions the **in-production (champion)** model declined, with their top feature drivers (for adverse-action reasons)
6. `incident.md` — a production incident to respond to
7. `fairness_slice.csv` / `.md` — **BONUS** only (Unit 13): candidate declines by region
8. `REPORT_TEMPLATE.md` — the memo structure to fill in
9. `DEFENSE-QUESTIONS.md` — the recorded-defense rules + the question bank (read before you record)

**Submit:** your completed memo **and** a 3-minute recorded defense. Read
`DEFENSE-QUESTIONS.md` first — it lists the question bank and how to pick your two
questions. See the assignment for the full rubric.
